# Upwork portfolio entry

## Title
AI Front Desk Automation: Website Chatbot + n8n for Clinics, Restaurants, Agencies and Hotels (Arabic/English)

## Description (about 150 words)
Small businesses lose hours every day to repetitive messages, and missed bookings cost real money. I built an AI front desk that handles customer messages 24/7 in Arabic and English.

A website chatbot talks to visitors and collects their booking. n8n then handles it the way *that* business needs. The clinic gets urgent alerts for patients in pain. The restaurant's kitchen is warned about allergies and large parties. The agency gets every lead scored Hot, Warm or Cold. The hotel sees the nights calculated and early arrivals flagged. Everything lands in Google Sheets, and staff are alerted on Telegram within seconds.

For the clinic it also answers price and opening-hour questions strictly from the clinic's own FAQ. It emails replies in the patient's language, sends reminders the day before (fewer no-shows) and a summary every evening.

It is built for production: a secured webhook, validated AI output with retries and fallbacks, instant error alerts, and no secrets in code.
